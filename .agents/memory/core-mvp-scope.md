---
name: Core Mizan MVP scope
description: Product constraints from the revised core wedding-planning brief.
---

The current MVP is a wedding-planning product, not primarily a vendor directory. Its five main sections are Dashboard, My Wedding, Budget, Vendors, and Invitations & Guests.

**Why:** The revised user brief explicitly prioritizes a cohesive planning experience over adding modules and supersedes the original broader competition brief.

**How to apply:** Do not reintroduce major assistant, support-program, family-permission, home-planning, moodboard, seating-chart, billing, or native-mobile modules without a new request. Profile/settings can remain subordinate controls. Preserve existing useful data and working features rather than replacing the app.

Quiz answers must influence the dashboard, checklist, starting budget and vendor recommendations. Invitations and guest RSVPs are a core acquisition channel; a shared invitation must work from a guest's separate browser, not merely simulate an RSVP in the couple's local storage.

**Why:** The user explicitly called these connected behaviors essential to the MVP, not decorative onboarding or isolated screens.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction between public invitation content and private wedding data. Keep fictional vendor profiles/prices/ratings clearly demo-labelled, and do not add real booking or payment behavior.
